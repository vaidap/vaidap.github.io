Portfolio

I've made 40+ experimental microgames on my itch.io over the last 10+ years. I believe in keeping scope small and focused.

I've freelanced as a UI/UX designer for Strange Scaffold on Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Tactical Takedown and Life Eater, and regularly provide UI/UX feedback on indie games.

I'm available for freelance UI/UX design and reviews, game design, and narrative work. Digital and analog games, and projects outside of games, are all welcome!
