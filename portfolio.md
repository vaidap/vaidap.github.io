# Portfolio

I've made 40+ experimental microgames on my [itch.io](https://vaidap.itch.io/) over the last 10+ years. I believe in keeping scope small and focused.

I've freelanced as a UI/UX designer for [Strange Scaffold](https://www.strangescaffold.com/) on [Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Tactical Takedown](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3229100/Teenage_Mutant_Ninja_Turtles_Tactical_Takedown/) and [Life Eater](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2632930/Life_Eater/), and regularly provide UI/UX feedback on indie games.

I'm available for freelance UI/UX design and reviews, game design, and narrative work. Digital and analog games, and projects outside of games, are all welcome!
